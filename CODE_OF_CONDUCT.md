# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect other people's time: search existing issues first, and keep reports specific.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information, including file names, paths or media from their libraries, without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider harmful. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** feature on the comment, or contact the maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
